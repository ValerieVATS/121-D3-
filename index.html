Escape the Planet

Craft a ladder from sticks to climb the grey mountains. Gather stone and iron there (iron appears 30% of the time). Build a twig bridge to cross lakes and gather water. Craft a beacon with 20 stone, 20 iron, and 10 water to summon a spaceship and escape!
